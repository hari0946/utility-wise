Alerts & Email

High and low limits with smart notifications

Temperature sensors and utility KPIs have configurable limits. When a parameter goes outside the allowed range, Utility Wise triggers alerts and email notifications to the configured team members.

Alert configuration

  • • High and low limits configured per tag or sensor group.
  • • Conditions based alerts (value, duration, rate of change).
  • • Clear separation between warning and critical alerts.
  • • Alarm acknowledgement and history for audit and analysis.

Email alerts

  • • Email alerts sent when configured conditions are met.
  • • Includes parameter name, current value, limit and timestamp.
  • • Can be addressed to operations, maintenance or utility teams.
  • • Helps reduce manual monitoring and response time.
